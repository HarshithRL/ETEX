## Page 4

- Our understanding of your request
<!-- loc page=4 block=b_adf23ad62a0e type=list -->

For executing its payments, Etex Group (henceforth: Etex) uses the systems of the Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunications ('Swift'). As part of the Swift Customer Security Programme ('CSP'), every Swift user (that is, a company connected to the Swift network and processing financial transactions via Swift) has to submit an annual Security Attestation, showing compliance levels with the security controls. The controls establish a security baseline for the entire Swift community. These controls must be implemented by all users on their local Swift infrastructure, depending on the type of architecture.
<!-- loc page=4 block=b_d3e6c03c9cd1 type=text -->

We understand that Etex plans to perform an independent assessment of the security controls according to the Swift Customer Security Controls Framework (CSCF). The Swift system and operational processes in scope support the main business processes. We understand that Etex's Swift environment consists of two separate Bank Identifier Codes (BIC), which are supported by a separate IT environment each requiring a separate independent assessment. Consequently, a thorough understanding of the security posture and operational resilience of each IT environment is required.
<!-- loc page=4 block=b_5aab792d4234 type=text -->

The purpose of the Swift independent assessments is to deliver a report to Etex that fully complies with all Swift requirements, enabling the firm to develop its own unbiased perspective and further enhance its IT internal control environment.
<!-- loc page=4 block=b_6a4813a5fa8e type=text -->

In order to fulfill this objective, Etex plans to engage an external assessor with demonstrated expertise and experience in performing Swift CSP assessments. The assessor will support Etex in the process of interpreting the Swift controls and guidelines, defining (risk-based) test scripts, and providing guidance on the documentation and evidence to be collected to meet the (minimum) requirements of the Swift CSP.
<!-- loc page=4 block=b_decae55f1ede type=text -->

We understand that Etex wishes the assessor to rely on external assurance that can be provided by third parties as much as possible, to reduce the workload for the assessor. Investigating this should be explicitly included in the initial scoping discussions and agreed upon between the assessor.
<!-- loc page=4 block=b_88ef0884c71e type=text -->

We understand respecting the timeline of the engagement is important in this assignment. The timeline from kick-off to initial reporting should be carried out between August and September 2026. Given the holiday period, this timeline will require strict follow-up, which we include in our proposed methodology. In case we identify gaps in the initial assessment report, Etex will need time to remediate those gaps, which should be done in October and November 2026. In that case, we will perform a final re-assessment of the non-compliant areas to validate that the gaps have been closed. This final reassessment reporting must be delivered to Etex no later than mid-end November 2026, in order to allow sufficient time for Etex to submit the assessment in the KYC Security Attestation (KYC- SA) platform by end of November 2026. The official deadline imposed by Swift is end of December 2026.
<!-- loc page=4 block=b_618055931bee type=text -->

In any case, throughout our fieldwork, we operate using an iterative and transparent approach: any observations we identify during fieldwork are communicated to and discussed with you iteratively during the workshops to enable prompt corrective actions and ensure we meet the established timeline. The detailed planning will be further customized in consultation with Etex.
<!-- loc page=4 block=b_d5626f294bc2 type=text -->
